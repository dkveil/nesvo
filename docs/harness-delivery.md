Implemented: the owner's definition is delivered and every cited test passed in CI or the native nightly.
Partial: part of the definition is missing, unproven or needs a live provider or device; the caveat names who closes it.
Pending: not started. Evidence is the strongest single proof, usually a test name; "none yet" means none exists.
